## Separation rule

Write each conclusion in three parts when possible:

1. Evidence: what the GIS metric measured, with units and provenance.
2. Cultural reading: which traditional metaphor the evidence can support under the selected lens.
3. Boundary: what the evidence cannot establish.

Traditional terms vary by school, region, period, and practitioner. If the user supplies a school or textual source, state that lens. Otherwise label the mapping as a generic, simplified cultural interpretation and avoid presenting it as a consensus definition.
